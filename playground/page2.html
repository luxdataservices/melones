Melones Italo

Four tracks of chiptune Italo disco, played by the Melones firmware code compiled for your browser: SID bass, arps and leads, a SID drum kit, kick ducking and the INFINITY CLAP. Nothing here touches your FM-1.

Song follows the arrangement. Tap a section to queue it: it starts when the current 4 bars end.

Play it

Press Play, or the space bar. Click steps to add or remove notes; drums have a row per drum. Click a lane's name area to choose which track the keyboard and the sound knobs control. Computer keys: Z S X D C V… is the lower octave, Q 2 W 3 E R… the upper.

Theme swaps the chords, bassline and lead melody together, so every part agrees, and Key moves all of it. The drum lane's second menu picks the beat: Italo, Spacer (transcribed from your Spacer Woman loop, with its bap-bap-bap claps closing every second bar), or 1986 (transcribed from your 1986 loop). Each melodic lane's Held key menu turns one held key into a part: an octave bounce on the bass, a chord or a chord arpeggio (built in the song's key) on the others. Latch keeps it going when you let go; Steps off silences the lane's pattern so you can play it live. Solo plays one lane alone (shift-click to solo several). DUCK on a lane is how hard the kick pushes it down; DUCK REL is how fast it comes back. The INFINITY CLAP sends each clap into a huge room: SWELL plays the start of that room backwards so it rises into the next clap, FEED sets how long the wash hangs (all the way to forever), WET how loud it all is, and EVERY picks which claps get it (every 4th: the last clap of each two bars).

Keep a sound

Tweak the selected track's sound, name it, and copy it. Paste it to Claude and it goes into the Melones firmware.
