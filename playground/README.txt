MELONES ITALO: the playground on your laptop

Quickest: double-click "Melones Italo.html". It opens in your browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox)
and runs entirely on your machine. Click Play (or press the space bar).

As its own app window:
  1. pip install pywebview        (once)
  2. python3 melones_app.py

Notes
- Sound starts on your first click: browsers don't let pages make sound before you touch them.
- The synth is the Melones firmware code compiled to WebAssembly, so it sounds like the FM-1 build.
- Nothing here connects to or changes your FM-1.
